# ReChord 1.9.2.9

- **Qobuz and MusicBrainz add-on support.** ReChord sends the track title and artist when available to help compatible add-ons find the right recording. Existing query-only add-ons still receive the usual search query.
- **Keep your audio in Convert to Video.** The video plays alongside the audio you already have. If you’re listening in lossless FLAC, it stays lossless.
- **Better video fit on tall and wide screens.** The inline video fits above the audio details, keeping them visible.
- **Faster video setup.** ReChord looks up the video while preparing playback sync.
- **Cleaner Home and customization.** Shelves dedicated to a single artist or album are hidden from Home and its customization menu. General recommendations, playlists, and featured cards remain available.
