# ReChord 1.9.2.8

### Home and discovery

- Featured includes more song recommendations based on your listening history. It mixes genres when listening stats have genre tags, filters duplicate covers and releases, and plays the song each card shows.
- Home shows cached Featured cards while it refreshes. On a cold start, ready cards appear after a short wait, and more can arrive while you stay on Home.
- Manage Home sections in Settings. The broader new releases catalog is available in Explore.

### Playback and USB audio

- Autoplay continues the current radio when possible and keeps recommendations behind your playlist and manually queued tracks. **Play Picks** can continue the artist radio and resume when a new batch arrives after the last track ends.
- USB Direct can reuse the connection between tracks with matching PCM formats. Switching from USB Direct to Android 14 USB restores the DAC's prior hardware volume when supported.
- Audio Pipeline shows **PCM Preserved** and **Bit-perfect output** when the USB path verifies unchanged PCM.

### Local Music and diagnostics

- Sort Albums, Artists, Album Artists, Folders, and Playlists from the active tab. Songs search returns tracks, including matches by album or artist name.
- Use **Settings → Diagnostics** to export a redacted log or open a prefilled GitHub issue after a problem. Reports are shared only when you choose to send them.
