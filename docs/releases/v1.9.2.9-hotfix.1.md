# ReChord 1.9.2.9 Hotfix 1

- Convert to Video keeps the audio that is already playing. If you’re listening in FLAC, the video opens without switching you to Opus.
- The video fits the space available on tall and wide screens, with the audio details shown below it.
- ReChord looks up the video while preparing playback sync, reducing the work that has to happen in sequence.
- Home and its settings hide shelves dedicated to a single artist or album. General recommendations, playlists, and featured cards remain available.

- Custom addons now receive the track title and artist when available, helping compatible sources such as Qobuz and MusicBrainz find the right recording.
