# ReChord 1.10.7

- **Automatic bit-perfect audio across USB DACs and DAPs.** ReChord now detects your built-in hi-res headphone jack or USB DAC automatically and selects the cleanest output path—including Direct Hi-Res PCM for dedicated music players like the HiBy R6 III and Android 14+ bit-perfect USB audio—while remembering device capabilities across restarts.
- **Instant USB driver switching & cleaner unplug handling.** Switching between Direct USB and Android 14+ USB updates the active song immediately without waiting for the next track. Unplugging a USB DAC falls back cleanly without stalling, and canceling an exclusive USB access request no longer loops the permission prompt.
- **Safe, 100 / 100 Bit-Perfect software volume & Now Playing volume bar.** USB DACs with hardware volume keep their existing level on connect. DACs without hardware volume automatically engage software volume at a safe 50% startup cap (remembered per DAC) while preserving 100 / 100 Bit-Perfect fidelity. The floating volume bar now appears smoothly inside Now Playing with consistent 5% steps, safe-area support, and Reduce Motion / Light theme polish.
- **Clearer Audio Fidelity & Audio Pipeline details.** Redesigned the Fidelity card with distinct Verdict and color-coded Output Path badges (*Bit-Perfect*, *Full-Resolution*, *High-Precision USB/Wired/HDMI*, *Bluetooth Wireless*), concise footer notes, and accurate badges for Android 14+ USB and built-in DAP hi-res.
- **Simpler Audio Settings & polished Appearance layout.** Streamlined playback controls into one **Bit-Perfect Output** toggle with a smoothly animated **Advanced audio** drawer, clearer **Built-in DAC / Wired output** and precision options, icons for nested settings rows, and **Appearance → Home** grouped cleanly above **Now Playing layout**.
- **Cross-path reliability fixes.** Fixed **32-bit Float** precision selection, HDMI eARC and Digital Line Out detection, DAP end-of-track drains, USB tail completion, and crossfade preloading overwriting the active track's Audio Pipeline status.
