# ReChord 1.10.6

- **Smoother Direct USB DAC playback.** Improved the Direct USB audio driver for cleaner, stutter-free playback across 16-bit, 24-bit, and 32-bit tracks up to 384 kHz—including smoother track skips, faster clock locking, and automatic recovery from brief USB interruptions when switching apps or turning off the screen.
- **No more slow-motion video interference.** When you pause ReChord or play a video in another app like Facebook, Instagram, or Messenger, ReChord hands the USB DAC back within half a second so videos play normally, then resumes cleanly when you return.
- **Reliable 24-bit and high-rate resume.** Fixed playback fallbacks and buffering stalls when resuming 24-bit or high-sample-rate tracks after a pause or during slower network track switches.
- **Single USB permission prompt.** Plugging in or returning to ReChord with a USB DAC now shows a single clean permission prompt instead of repeating it.
