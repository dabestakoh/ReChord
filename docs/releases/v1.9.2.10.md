# ReChord 1.9.2.10

- **Choose your Now Playing layout.** Keep Default or switch to Vinyl or Poster from **Settings → Appearance → Now Playing layout** or by holding the top handle in Now Playing. Lyrics and Queue open within the selected layout.
- **Make Vinyl your own.** Choose black, album colors, or a custom color, then pick a record finish. Drag the tonearm to seek, or use the curved seekbar on Poster.
- **Lyrics in motion.** Poster animates timed words and overlapping vocals below the artwork. Expanded Lyrics offers Rise and glow, Shimmer sweep, Word lift, and Plain sweep.
- **More ways to find lyrics.** ReChord expands provider coverage and checks language and timing before using provider order to break ties. Embedded lyrics come first, and you can still select a provider manually.
- **Video in portrait and landscape.** Video can replace the album art while staying synchronized with the existing audio. The fullscreen control lets you enter or exit fullscreen in landscape.
- **Steadier player controls.** Vinyl and Poster have independent volume-bar settings. Format details, the one-line Automix status, and the quality badge have reserved space so playback controls stay anchored while a stream loads.
