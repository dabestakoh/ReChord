## ReChord 1.9.2.12

### Realistic Turntable Vinyl Experience
- **Synchronized swipe & Next/Previous choreography**: Swiping or tapping Next/Previous now follows real turntable order — the tonearm lifts and clears to the outer resting edge, the outgoing record lifts smoothly off the platter and slides away, the incoming record glides in solid (no darkening or shadow bleed-through) and drops straight down onto the platter, and only once seated does the tonearm glide onto the groove and start spinning the record.
- **Headshell-only tonearm touch target**: Restricted tonearm scrubbing to the headshell/cartridge head with a generous outer halo so swiping across the vinyl never accidentally triggers a seek.
- **Edge-to-edge Landscape Vinyl**: Extended the Landscape Vinyl stage all the way under the status bar and bottom taskbar/nav bar (matching Poster) without clipping when the record lifts and drops during animations.
- **Zero-redraw mini-player maximize**: Cached the Portrait vinyl geometry per screen size so expanding from the mini-player never redraws the vinyl texture.

### Performance, Smoothness & Thermal Optimizations
- **Single-layer flattened textured vinyls**: Pre-baked all patterned/textured vinyl finishes (`Marbled`, `Swirl`, `Galaxy`, `Glitter`, `Splatter`, `Smoked`, etc.) off the UI thread into a cached 1-layer raster `ImageBitmap`, plus background pre-baking for previous/next tracks so textured vinyls rotate as a single GPU quad with zero stutter and zero plain-circle flash on swipe.
- **Lighter Vinyl rendering**: Flattened radially symmetric finishes (`Plain`, `Translucent`, `Color-in-Color`, `Metallic`, `Gold Record`) into a single static offscreen platter+grooves layer, switched to a calm 28°/sec frame-accurate spin, replaced the heavy GPU tonearm blur buffer with a lightweight layered shadow, and eliminated per-frame drop-shadow gradient allocations.
- **Cooler, smoother Poster & Lyrics playback**: Paused the Poster curved rim visualizer automatically in the background, paced visualizer redraws and spectrum analysis to ~30–35 FPS, skipped off-screen previous/next artwork layers when idle, paced instrumental lyrics dot animations, and removed duplicate high-frequency lyric clocks.

### New Icon & Visual Polish
- **New "Obsidian Vinyl R" App Icon**: Redesigned the ReChord icon across all adaptive launcher, monochrome/themed, notification, Play Store, and brand vector assets with clean closed geometry, alongside a refreshed `README.md`.
- **UI & Stability Fixes**: Fixed pinched rounded corners in **Settings → Appearance**, fixed landscape vinyl center-label offset and tiny landscape Poster lyrics, and fixed a `StackOverflowError` during vinyl layer baking.
- **Preserved compatibility**: Existing app data, package identity, signing key, Bit-Perfect/DSP audio behavior, and query-only add-ons remain fully preserved.
