# ReChord 1.9.2.7

### Local Music

- **Faster library browsing.** Open your saved library while ReChord checks for new and changed files in the background. Use **Refresh Music Library** in the Sort/View menu to scan on demand.
- **More ways to browse.** Browse Songs, Albums, Artists, Album Artists, Folders, Playlists, and Recently Added. Artist and Album Artist pages lead to albums and tracks, with multi-disc albums in disc and track order.
- **Recently Added.** See up to 100 of the newest dated tracks. Choose newest first or alphabetical order from the Sort/View menu.
- **Letter jumps and search.** Tap or drag the A–Z rail in long alphabetical lists, with support for accented letters and other scripts. Albums, Artists, and Album Artists sort past a leading “The” while keeping the full name visible.
- **Better local covers.** ReChord can read embedded artwork and adjacent cover images, caches resized thumbnails, and supplies local covers to Android media controls.

### Playback and Replay

- **Audio Output** opens tall enough to show **Audio Pipeline** below the volume slider when space allows. Playback settings show **Standard audio output** when the built-in DAC exclusive request falls back.
- **More accurate Audio Pipeline readings.** Track changes no longer compare the new source with an AudioTrack reading from a different track. Android USB validates the current mixer settings when the output changes.
- **Safer Replay navigation.** Story pages and ranked lists handle live updates without relying on stale positions or duplicate row keys.
