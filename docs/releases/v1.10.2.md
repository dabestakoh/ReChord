## ReChord 1.10.2

This update makes music stored on your device easier to find and manage, and improves Now Playing on landscape screens. ReChord continues to be based on BitChord 1.10.

### Local Music

- Choose which folders to scan for music. You can select multiple folders, including supported external storage on car head units. Include subfolders if you want, and cancel a background scan at any time. Refresh your library on demand or clear the index while keeping your files.
- Browse by song, album, artist, album artist, folder, playlist, or Recently Added. Each tab remembers its scroll position and list or grid view. Tap the active tab again to return to the top, or use the A–Z index to jump through the list.
- Find high-resolution tracks with the **Hi-Res** filter and see which songs are marked Hi-Res at a glance.
- View detailed song information, edit tags, and select multiple songs to queue or add to a playlist. Hide tracks and restore them later, or choose to delete the files from your device.

### Landscape and car displays

- The main menu and mini-player now sit side by side when a track is active in landscape, leaving more room for your library. Controls stay clear of screen cutouts and Android navigation.
- Vinyl and Poster use a consistent Now Playing layout. Lyrics and Queue stay accessible at the bottom while either panel is open, and Queue keeps Shuffle, Repeat, and Autoplay within reach.
- Poster artwork fills more of the screen, especially on wide car displays. It extends to the left edge, and the curved seek bar keeps both elapsed time and total duration in view.
