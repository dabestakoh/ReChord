# ReChord 1.9.2.4

This update focuses on smoother playback, clearer audio information, and a more polished listening experience.

### What's new
- **Fidelity Score:** See whether your music keeps its original sample rate and bit depth on the way to your audio device.
- **Artwork-inspired colors:** Player controls, song information, and lyrics now match the current album artwork, with smoother transitions between tracks.
- **Refined player design:** Improved spacing, icons, and visual effects for a cleaner Now Playing screen.

### Improvements and fixes
- **More reliable playback controls:** Shuffle, Repeat, and AutoPlay settings stay consistent as you switch tracks, playlists, or reopen your queue.
- **Improved USB DAC handling:** More dependable audio output and clearer playback-status reporting.
- **Smoother transitions:** UI and playback refinements to help reduce interruptions and visual glitches.
- **Better defaults:** New installations use the updated audio preferences, while existing settings are preserved.

Thanks for listening with ReChord!
