## ReChord 1.9.2.11.1

### Performance & Thermal Optimizations
- **Cooler, smoother Vinyl playback**: Baked vinyl wax finishes into a cached offscreen layer, skipped off-screen previous/next vinyl discs in portrait unless swiping, cached the Hi-Fi tonearm shadow so it only re-blurs when the arm noticeably moves, and capped vinyl spin updates on 90Hz/120Hz displays for identical smoothness with far less GPU wakeups.
- **Cooler, smoother Poster playback**: Paused the curved rim visualizer automatically when the app is in the background, paced visualizer redraws and audio spectrum analysis to ~30–35 FPS with precomputed frequency bins, skipped off-screen previous/next artwork and mesh layers unless swiping, and paced instrumental lyrics dot animations while gating them on foreground visibility.
- **Same visuals & audio quality**: Zero visual regressions across all 16 Vinyl finishes, Poster expressive lyrics, and Default skin, with Bit-Perfect and DSP audio behavior completely preserved.
