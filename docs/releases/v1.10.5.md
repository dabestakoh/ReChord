# ReChord 1.10.5

- **Familiar & Discovery Autoplay.** Switch between **Familiar** (tracks and artists you already enjoy) and **Discovery** (fresh similar music) right inside Queue, with a full upcoming queue and instant **Start Radio** updates.
- **Durable Taste Memory & Queue Learning.** ReChord now remembers your likes, dislikes, early skips, dismissed Home cards, and queue actions across restarts (saved separately per account). Removing an Autoplay suggestion dials back that artist (and **Undo** reverses it), while tapping **Play Next** or **Add to Queue** counts as a positive vote without changing your active radio's vibe.
- **Rediscover, Time-of-Day Taste & Home Menus.** Home now surfaces **Rediscover** picks, adapts to your time-of-day listening habits, and supports long-press menus on Featured cards (including **Not interested**).
- **Search Add-ons Radio.** Start continuous radio playback directly from Search Add-ons results.
- **Instant Online Playlists & Liked Music Reordering.** Online playlists and Liked Songs open immediately from cache, stay in sync with pull-to-refresh and a subtle updating indicator, and support track reordering in **Liked Music**.
- **Settings-Aware Polish & Cleaner Icon.** Refined **Reduce Liquid Glass**, **Reduce Motion**, Light/Dark theme, and landscape layout behavior across screens, plus a cleaner, seam-free **ReChord** icon.
