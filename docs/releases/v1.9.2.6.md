# ReChord 1.9.2.6

### Playlists and playback

- **Import playlists by link.** Bring public playlists from Apple Music, Spotify, and YouTube Music into ReChord.
- **Faster Direct Source Stream preparation.** ReChord prepares source matches for the restored track before you press Play and resolves the next track earlier during playback. Resuming keeps your saved position. If the preferred source is unavailable or takes too long, playback can still fall back to YouTube.
- **More reliable playlist starts.** Fixed cancelled background lookups causing repeated buffering, retries, and unwanted track skips when using Direct Source Stream.

### Audio and USB DACs

- **Automatic output precision.** Auto is the default for new settings and selects precision according to the playing source and output support. Existing manual choices are preserved.
- **Clearer Audio Pipeline information.** Source, app output, verified DAC output, and device capabilities are distinguished. The fidelity score remains visible on speakers, Bluetooth, and USB outputs, with clearer conversion and fallback status.
- **USB playback improvements.** Updated the Direct USB library to libusb 1.0.30, improved startup and transfer handling, and preserved the active USB output during quality upgrades. Android USB requests compatible bit-perfect formats when the system offers them and reports standard output when it does not.

### Now Playing

- **Audio controls together.** Listen Together and Spatial audio are now available below Cast and above volume in Audio Output. Spatial audio uses the same saved setting as Playback Settings.
- **Cleaner navigation.** Removed the separate Listen Together player icon and its visibility setting. Opening Listen Together now waits for Now Playing to close so the page fade is visible.
