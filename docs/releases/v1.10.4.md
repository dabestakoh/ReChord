## ReChord 1.10.4

ReChord 1.10.4 refines the Now Playing experience across car head-units, digital audio players (DAPs), and phones, while making Local Music artwork caching faster and smarter. ReChord continues to be based on BitChord 1.10.

### Now Playing & Car Head-Units / DAPs

- **Poster layout:** Lyrics on landscape screens (including car head-units) no longer clip at the right or bottom edges. The title, artist, and playback source sit higher to give lyrics more room so 2–3 lines are comfortably readable, and verses that previously skipped their second line now display completely in both Portrait and Landscape.
- **Vinyl layout:** Landscape controls and song details now sit vertically centered with balanced spacing instead of leaving a large empty gap. On compact DAP screens, the vinyl tonearm needle is much easier to grab and scrub without interfering with track swipes.
- **Default layout:** Added smooth cover-swipe animations in landscape, improved artwork proportions and top-edge framing on shorter DAP screens so album covers aren't cut off at the top, and kept bright or white album covers from washing out text and playback controls.
- **Disable blend effects:** Turning on **Disable blend effects** now applies cleanly across Default, Vinyl, and Poster, keeping player icons, text, sliders, and lyrics in crisp plain white.

### Local Music

- **Faster, one-time artwork caching:** Local album artwork now prepares faster in the background and continues smoothly even when the screen turns off. Once your library's artwork is checked, caching will not re-run on its own unless you add or update music files—and only new or changed tracks are processed.
