# ReChord 1.9.2.1

Welcome to the first public release of **ReChord**! Enjoy a refreshed music player with smoother browsing and improved playlist handling.

## What's New

- **A fresh ReChord look** — updated app name, logo, and Home screen.
- **Faster playlist opening** — get back to your saved playlists without waiting for everything to load again.
- **Smoother browsing** — improvements to scrolling on Home and in music playlists.
- **Better playlist sorting** — sort by date added or song title, and keep your chosen order when navigating.
- **More reliable playlist changes** — improvements when adding songs, organizing tracks, and refreshing playlists.
- **More music source flexibility** — improved support for compatible custom add-ons.
- **Various fixes and refinements** — smaller usability improvements throughout the app.

## Download & Install

Choose the **ReChord** APK below for your Android device. If there is a universal APK, it is the easiest choice for most users. Most modern Android phones can also use the `arm64-v8a` version.

**Note:** ReChord Preview and ReChord Release are separate apps and keep separate data. To update an existing Release installation, you must use an APK signed with the same certificate. Back up important app data before uninstalling any previous version.

Thank you for trying ReChord! You can report bugs or suggest features in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dabestakoh/ReChord/issues).

*ReChord is an independent fork of BitChord. Third-party music availability and audio capabilities depend on your source and device.*
