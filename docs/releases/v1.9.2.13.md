## ReChord 1.9.2.13

### Adaptive Landscape, DAP & 720p Car Head-Unit UI
- **2-column Landscape Layout & Vinyl Skin Pickers**: On landscape phones, DAPs, and 720p car head-units, **Now Playing layout** and **Vinyl appearance** now switch to a 2-column layout with height-capped previews, immediately tappable **Default / Vinyl / Poster** tabs and **Cancel / Apply** buttons, and an adaptive 3–5 column vinyl finish grid.
- **Adaptive Landscape Now Playing Controls**: Added `AdaptivePlaybackPillRow` so all 5–6 transport controls (`Previous`, `Play/Pause`, `Next`, `Favorite`, `More`, `Rotate`) scale cleanly to fit narrow DAP widths (`560–680 dp`) without clipping, and exposed the rotate button in compact portrait mode as well.
- **Aligned & Clickable Landscape Poster**: Unified Landscape Poster's right column with Landscape Vinyl (Bold Title/Artist → flexible interactive lyrics band → 6-button controls → Stats/Volume/Quality/Actions) and narrowed the Landscape sheet-handle hitbox so controls are always 100% clickable.
- **Compact Car Browse Chrome & Adaptive Popups**: On wide, short landscape screens (`>= 700 dp` wide, `< 500 dp` tall), the bottom navigation bar and mini-player sit side-by-side to reclaim vertical space for Settings, Home, and Library. `Audio Pipeline`, `Cast`, `App Language`, and `Translation Language` dialogs now cap their scrollable bodies dynamically against screen height so action buttons never clip off-screen.

### Vinyl Realism & Maximize Animation Polish
- **Brushed-Silver Tonearm & Matte-Black Headshell**: Redesigned the Vinyl tonearm and plinth with smooth cylindrical brushed-silver shading, a satin matte-black headshell and cartridge, and zero harsh perimeter outlines.
- **Final-from-Frame-1 Vinyl Texture**: Locked patterned vinyl seeds to immutable track IDs so async metadata backfills never redraw or swap the vinyl texture when the tonearm lands.
- **Synchronized Mini-Player → Now Playing Animation**: Fixed the flying cover curve on maximize/minimize to ease out onto the live rising sheet position and finish Poster's curved mask before handoff, eliminating album-art lag on Vinyl and curved-edge doubling on Poster.

### Typography, Settings & Stability Fixes
- **Bold Song Titles Across All Skins**: Made Now Playing song titles consistently bold across Default, Vinyl, and Poster in both Portrait and Landscape.
- **Aligned Settings Rows & Text Boundaries**: Fixed iconless row indentation, aligned nested sub-rows and dividers cleanly, added a `16.dp` right-side gutter between text and trailing switches/chevrons, and added clean ellipsis truncation for long subtitles such as *Lyrics sources*.
- **Listen Together Fallback**: Restored fallback to the default Listen Together server when the build-time server secret is blank.
