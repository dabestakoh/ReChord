# ReChord 1.9.2.2

This update makes playlists feel more responsive and keeps your saved music more reliably in sync with YouTube Music.

## What's New

- **Smarter playlist updates** — quickly checks for changes when you open a saved playlist, without downloading the entire playlist unnecessarily.
- **Faster access to your songs** — previously opened playlists still appear immediately.
- **More reliable refreshes** — better handling of temporary connection problems, with existing cached songs kept available.
- **Improved change detection** — recognizes differences in song counts and available duration information.
- **Fewer unnecessary refreshes** — avoids repeated requests when playlist details are unchanged or unavailable.
- **Additional stability improvements** — better handling of playlist information and background checks.

## Download & Install

Download the APK for your Android device below. The universal APK is the easiest choice for most devices; most modern Android phones can also use the `arm64-v8a` APK.

If updating an existing ReChord Release installation, Android requires an APK signed with the same certificate. ReChord Preview and Release are separate apps with separate data. Back up important app data before uninstalling either version.

Thank you for using ReChord! Share bug reports and suggestions in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dabestakoh/ReChord/issues).

*Availability of music, streaming formats and audio behavior depends on your music sources and device.*
