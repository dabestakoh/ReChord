## ReChord 1.10.1

ReChord 1.10.1 brings the latest **BitChord 1.10** features to ReChord along with smoother Vinyl and Poster animations, a cleaner Audio Output menu, and automatic romanized lyrics.

### What's New from BitChord 1.10
- **Replay Year Stories & Shareable Cards**: Relive your listening year with interactive story slides and share your top music highlights directly as cards or videos.
- **Jump to the "Best Part" of a Song**: Tap the new **Best Part** button on the album cover in Now Playing to jump straight to a song's highlight moment (can be turned on or off in Settings).
- **Automix Cover & Ambient Artwork Transitions**: When Automix blends from one song into the next, your album cover and background can now smoothly crossfade along with the music.
- **Easier Queue Management**: Swipe songs in your queue or tap **⋯ → Select songs** to quickly select multiple tracks to remove or add to a playlist at once, complete with an undo button if you change your mind.
- **Playlist Search & Quick Settings Tile**: Filter inside playlists to find songs faster, and control playback from your phone's Quick Settings panel.
- **Smoother Player Controls & Song Credits**: Enjoy more responsive Play/Pause and Skip button animations, plus an updated song credits and details view.

### Vinyl & Poster Refinements (Portrait, Landscape, DAPs & Car Displays)
- **Natural Landscape Vinyl Swiping**: When swiping between tracks in Landscape Vinyl, the next or previous record now glides in smoothly from the edge of the screen instead of popping in abruptly.
- **Cleaner Landscape Poster Animation**: Opening and closing Now Playing in Landscape Poster now keeps the curved poster shape throughout the transition instead of showing a square cover in the middle of the screen.
- **Smooth Mini-Player Transitions Across All Vinyl Styles**: Album art now animates smoothly between the mini-player and Now Playing across every Vinyl finish (including Classic Black, Splatter, Liquid, and Picture Disc) as well as Poster mode.
- **Subtle Top Drag Handle**: Replaced the thick colored top bar in Vinyl and Poster with a slim, subtle translucent handle tucked near the top edge so it never gets in the way of song titles while staying just as easy to swipe down.
- **Better Lyrics Space on Short Screens**: Improved the Landscape Poster layout on compact DAPs and car screens so interactive lyrics always have enough room to read and tap.

### Lyrics & Audio Output Improvements
- **Automatic Romanized Lyrics**: Non-Latin lyrics (such as Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, Thai, Russian, and Arabic) can now automatically show romanized pronunciation underneath each line so you can sing along right away. Turned on by default for new installs, and easily adjustable anytime in **Settings → Look & feel → Auto-romanize lyrics**.
- **Cleaner Audio Output & Spatial Audio Menu**: Opening **Audio Pipeline** from the **Audio Output** menu now neatly closes the Audio Output window first so menus never stack on top of each other. **Spatial Audio** now uses a clean checkmark indicator and clearly shows **"Spatial Audio is ON"** when active.
- **Stats for Nerds on New Installs**: **Stats for nerds** is now enabled by default on fresh installs so audio format details are visible right out of the box (existing installs keep your current setting).
