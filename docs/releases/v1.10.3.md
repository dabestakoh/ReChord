## ReChord 1.10.3

ReChord 1.10.3 makes Local Music steadier and artwork more reliable, with smoother browsing and quicker playlist updates. ReChord continues to be based on BitChord 1.10.

### Local Music

- New or changed music files are added in the background while your library stays available. Returning from an online song no longer starts another scan or repeats artwork caching when your music folders have not changed.
- Album covers are prepared throughout your library, including artwork embedded in music files. Supported images in album folders are still used when needed, and changed covers are picked up.
- Sort each view your way. Songs, albums, artists, and folders remember their own choices, with A–Z as the usual default. Recently Added starts with the newest tracks, and a new sort appears right away.
- In Folders, SD cards and other external drives show their name instead of a storage code. Unnamed drives show **External Storage**.

### Browsing and playlists

- Artwork in lists and other screens uses less memory, and visual effects do less work while navigating. The look stays familiar while browsing feels steadier on slower devices.
- Adding an online song to a playlist responds sooner and shows the updated playlist promptly.
