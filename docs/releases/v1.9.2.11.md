# ReChord 1.9.2.11

- **Landscape Vinyl and Landscape Poster.** Rotating sideways now opens dedicated widescreen layouts for Vinyl and Poster instead of reverting to Default. Landscape Vinyl shows the full uncropped spinning record with a tucked tonearm and centered controls, while Landscape Poster bleeds edge-to-edge on the left with a rising curved progress bar and centered widescreen controls. A quick **Rotate** button above `⋯` lets you flip between portrait and landscape on demand even with auto-rotate off.
- **Revamped Vinyl Studio and 16 realistic finishes.** Customize Vinyl with a live spinning showcase, color mode bar, tap-to-pick primary/accent swatches, and a visual 3-column mini-record grid. All 13 existing finishes now feature pressed-vinyl groove bands, run-out gloss, and beveled edges, joined by **Pinwheel**, **Tri-Color**, and **Gold Record**. Center album artwork is now unobstructed without a spindle dot.
- **Hi-Fi Vinyl tonearm and smoother animations.** The tonearm gains a brushed-metal counterweight, machined gimbal base, curved finger lift, soft drop shadow over light album art, 3-second progress peek on track start, and subtle needle lift on pause. Both Vinyl and Poster add bouncy spring pill buttons and smooth background color melts across track changes.
- **Expressive Poster lyrics and curved rim visualizer.** Poster lyrics now match full word-by-word motion with smooth title-to-lyrics transitions, a 3-second song-start rim peek, a 1.4-second post-scrub settle, and an optional **Curved rim visualizer** under **Settings → Appearance → Now Playing Layout → Poster**.
- **Unified full-width Queue and Lyrics.** Opening Queue or Lyrics in Vinyl or Poster now uses the full screen width from the first frame with the same compact horizontal bottom bar — eliminating width snapping on scroll.
- **Per-song lyrics timing and one-tap Auto-sync.** **Lyrics offset** now saves independently per song so fixing one track never shifts others, and includes a one-tap **Auto-sync** button with Undo that listens to vocals in the background to align lyrics automatically. The provider picker also distinguishes **API key required** and **Unavailable** from genuine misses.
- **Cleaner Appearance settings.** Settings under **Appearance** are now grouped into **App Appearance**, **Now Playing Skin**, and **Lyrics (All Skins)**, with Default, Vinyl, and Poster options kept inside their own skin tabs.
- **Video Mode fixes.** Improved audio-first video synchronization in Default mode, automatically exits Video Mode with a notice when no music video is available (no more stuck black box or ghost window after rotating), and keeps Vinyl and Poster free of video mode prompts.
